About Me

I’m Saksham Garg, a Product & UI/UX Designer focused on designing intuitive digital products that simplify complex workflows and create meaningful user experiences.

My work revolves around fintech platforms, SaaS dashboards, and e-commerce experiences—where usability, clarity, and structured interaction design play a critical role.

I’ve worked on products like investment platforms, operational dashboards, restaurant ordering systems, and Shopify-based websites, translating complex requirements into clean and scalable interfaces.

I focus on building solutions that balance user needs, business goals, and technical feasibility—ensuring every design decision adds value to the overall product experience.

I enjoy working on data-driven systems and workflow-heavy products, collaborating with teams to create efficient, intuitive, and impactful digital experiences.
