The Problem

Ordering food
shouldn't feel like work.

Too many options at once → decision fatigue from the first screen.

Customisation is buried → users can't easily change size, crust, or toppings.

Offers are invisible → deals hidden deep in menus nobody scrolls to.

Checkout feels long → too many steps, unclear pricing, drop-off risk.

The Solution

Simple flow.
No confusion.

Step-by-step guided flow — one decision at a time, nothing overwhelming.

Tap-to-customise — size, crust, and toppings right on the product screen.

Smart offer nudges — deals shown at the right moment, not buried away.

Fast, transparent checkout — clear bill breakdown, one tap to confirm.
