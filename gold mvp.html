06 — UX Decisions

The thinking behind the design

01
Vault or delivery — decided early.

The split happens at checkout step 1, not post-payment. Removes confusion, sets expectation upfront.

02
Checkout simplified aggressively.

Magento's default multi-page checkout reduced to a 3-step flow. Financial context, not e-commerce conventions.

03
Orders and transactions kept separate.

Orders = physical events (bought, delivered). Transactions = financial log. Same data, different mental models.

04
Dashboard: clarity over completeness.

First login shows "Your Gold" — a simplified view. Advanced modules are one tab deeper, not hidden.

05
Savings setup cut from 7 steps to 4.

Amount + frequency combined. Payment method deferred to last step. Less cognitive load at entry.

06
Trust built into the product, not bolted on.

Purity info on PDP. Vault certificate refs in dashboard. Third-party logos at checkout. Woven into the flow.
